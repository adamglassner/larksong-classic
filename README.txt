DAYBREAK CLASSIC
================

An alarm clock for Windows 95, 98, Me, 2000, XP, Vista, 7, 8, 8.1, 10 and 11.

Installing
----------
No installer is needed. Copy Daybreak.exe to any folder (for example
C:\Program Files\Daybreak Classic) and double-click it. To have it start
automatically, open Settings and tick "Start Daybreak when Windows starts".

Daybreak keeps running in the notification area (next to the clock) when you
close its window, so your alarms still ring. To quit, right-click the icon
and choose Exit. A computer that is asleep or switched off can't ring.

What it does
------------
* Clock: analog and digital, with seconds, and up to 6 world clocks
* Alarms: repeat days, names, snooze, your own WAV sound, and volume
  that rises gradually
* Up before your alarm? Click "Dismiss next ring" on the Alarms page,
  or right-click the tray icon and choose "Dismiss next alarm"
* Bedtime reminder, with an optional matching wake-up alarm
* Stopwatch with laps, and a timer
* Themes: Classic, Dark, Terminal (green on black), Clock radio and Retro
* Back up and restore alarms (the backup file also works in Daybreak
  on Android, Windows 10/11 and Linux)
* Windows 7 and later: weather for your city, and update checks

Settings are kept in daybreak.ini, in your Application Data folder (or next
to Daybreak.exe on Windows 95/98 without user profiles).

Weather data from Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com).
